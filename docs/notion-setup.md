# Notion Setup 

This directory outlines the setup and integration of the Notion workspace designed to complement this self-taught English study repository.

## Structure Overview
* **Languages:** Central hub displaying target languages, current level, and active goals.
* **To do list:** Kanban board tracking daily tasks, assignments, and study milestones (*To do*, *In progress*, *Done*).
* **Resources & Media:** Database for managing coursebooks, reading lists, articles, and external study links.
* **Flashcards:** Vocabulary bank for recording new words, phrasal verbs, translations, difficulties, and review dates.
* **Writing & Practice:** Section dedicated to essays, writing guides, and active language production.
