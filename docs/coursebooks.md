# Recommended English Coursebooks (A1 to C2)

A curated list of leading English Language Teaching (ELT) coursebooks and exam preparation series, organized by CEFR proficiency levels.


## 2. Complete IELTS Series Breakdown (B1 – C1)

Published by **Cambridge University Press**, the *Complete IELTS* series integrates general language development with specific IELTS test strategies.

* **Complete IELTS Bands 4–5 (CEFR B1 / Lower-Intermediate)**
  * **Focus:** Introduces key exam question types for all 4 papers (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking).
  * **Target:** Building core grammar, fundamental vocabulary, and initial test-taking confidence.
* **Complete IELTS Bands 5–6.5 (CEFR B2 / Intermediate – Upper-Intermediate)**
  * **Focus:** Tackles common exam mistake areas, essay structures (Task 2), and structured speaking responses.
  * **Target:** Standard undergraduate admission requirements and international employment standards.
* **Complete IELTS Bands 6.5–7.5 (CEFR C1 / Advanced)**
  * **Focus:** Advanced academic discourse, complex sentence transformation, subtle listening nuances, and high-band writing techniques.
  * **Target:** Postgraduate university admissions, medical registration, and professional licensing.
