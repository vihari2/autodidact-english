# English Books by CEFR Level

## A1 (total beginner)
- *The Very Hungry Caterpillar* – Eric Carle
- *The Cat in the Hat* – Dr. Seuss
- *Charlotte's Web* – E.B. White
- *Peter Pan* – J.M. Barrie
- *The Little Prince* – Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
- *Jack and the Beanstalk* (traditional tale)
- *Hello, Harvest Moon* – Ralph Fletcher

## A2
- *Charlie and the Chocolate Factory* – Roald Dahl
- *James and the Giant Peach* – Roald Dahl
- *Matilda* – Roald Dahl
- *The Secret Garden* – Frances Hodgson Burnett
- *The Adventures of Tom Sawyer* – Mark Twain
- Comic strips (Garfield, Calvin and Hobbes)
- *Aru Shah and the End of Time* – Roshani Chokshi

## B1 — current level, start here
- *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone* – J.K. Rowling 
- *Paper Towns* – John Green
- *The Fault in Our Stars* – John Green
- *Mortal Instruments: City of Bones* – Cassandra Clare
- *Ready Player One* – Ernest Cline
- *Divergent* – Veronica Roth
- *And Then There Were None* – Agatha Christie
- *Percy Jackson and the Olympians* – Rick Riordan
- *The Seventh Son* (Tales of Alvin Maker) – Orson Scott Card

## B2
- *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* – Lewis Carroll
- *The Hobbit* – J.R.R. Tolkien
- *Memoirs of a Geisha* – Arthur Golden
- *The Road* – Cormac McCarthy
- *The Cruel Prince* – Holly Black
- *The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe* – C.S. Lewis
- *The Help* ("Maids and Ladies") – Kathryn Stockett

## C1 (advanced)
- *Pride and Prejudice* – Jane Austen
- *A Game of Thrones* – George R.R. Martin
- *A Tale of Two Cities* – Charles Dickens
- *The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes* – Arthur Conan Doyle
- *The Diary of Anne Frank*
- *Jane Eyre* – Charlotte Brontë (one source places this at advanced-only)
- *Red Queen* – Victoria Aveyard
- *Gone Girl* ("Lost") – Gillian Flynn

## C2 (fluent / near-native)
- *Lolita* – Vladimir Nabokov
- *The Handmaid's Tale* – Margaret Atwood
- *Little Women* – Louisa May Alcott
- *Mortal Instruments: City of Bones* (also listed here by another source)
