# Self-Taught English Writing & Journaling Guide

A guide designed to help self-taught learners improve their English writing skills through daily journaling, reflection prompts, and vocabulary expansion.

---

## 1. How to Use Journaling to Practice English

* **Don't stress about mistakes:** Focus first on fluency (getting your ideas down) before correcting grammar.
* **Use a dictionary actively:** When you don't know a word, look it up in English and write it down.
* **Practice target structures:** Try using new grammar tenses (e.g., Past Simple for past stories, *going to* / *will* for future goals).
* **Keep entries short:** Aim for 5 to 15 minutes or 50–150 words per day to build a sustainable habit.

---

## 2. Daily Prompts for Beginners (A1 – A2)

* Describe your morning routine step-by-step.
* What are 3 things you are grateful for today and why?
* Describe your favorite food, how it tastes, and where you eat it.
* Describe your workspace or bedroom in detail.
* Write about your family members or a close friend.

---

## 3. Emotional & Reflection Prompts (B1 – C1)

### Analyzing Current Emotions & Situations
* What are the main emotions you are feeling right now?
* Write down a recent situation as if you were telling it to a friend from an outside perspective.
* Is there anything you wish you had done differently recently? Why or why not?
* Write a "goodbye letter" to a habit or situation you want to let go of.

### Managing Anxiety & Goal Setting
* What is a worst-case scenario you are worrying about, and how likely is it to actually happen?
* What is one small action you can take today to move closer to your main goal?
* Examine your internal self-talk: What have you been saying to yourself lately, and how does it affect you?

---

## 4. Useful Linking Words & Vocabulary

| Function | Transition Words / Phrases |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Adding Ideas** | *In addition, Furthermore, Also, Besides* |
| **Showing Contrast** | *However, Although, On the other hand, Even though* |
| **Giving Reasons** | *Because of, Due to, As a result, Therefore* |
| **Expressing Opinion** | *In my opinion, From my perspective, I believe that* |
