# Google Meet Solo Speaking Guide

This guide outlines how to use Google Meet for self-directed speaking practice to build fluency, confidence, and spontaneous sentence structure without the pressure of an audience.

---

## Objectives
* Overcome hesitation and fear of speaking out loud.
* Practice real-time sentence construction and vocabulary retrieval.
* Record sessions to evaluate pronunciation, pacing, and recurring grammar errors.

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Start a Call:**
   * Go to [meet.google.com](https://meet.google.com) and click **New Meeting ➔ Start an instant meeting**.
2. **Turn On Live Captions:**
   * Click the **Captions (CC)** button at the bottom.
   * *Why?* Live captions help you check if Google understands your pronunciation in real time.
3. **Record / Take Notes (Optional):**
   * Keep a notepad or your Notion **Google Meet** log open alongside the window to jot down words you struggled to recall.

---

## Recommended Routine (5–10 Minutes)

* **Minute 1–2 (Warm-up):** Describe your immediate surroundings, what you did today, or how you are feeling.
* **Minute 3–7 (Topic Focus):** Pick one prompt (from the list below) and speak continuously about it. Avoid long pauses—if you don't know a word, explain it using simpler English (*circumlocution*).
* **Minute 8–10 (Wrap-up & Review):** Note down any vocabulary gaps or grammar doubts to look up later.

---

## Speaking Topics & Prompts

### 🟩 Beginner (A1–A2)
* **Hobbies & Free Time:** What do you like to do on weekends? Why do you enjoy it?
* **Travel & Places:** Describe your favorite city or a place you would love to visit.
* **People:** Describe your best friend or a family member (their appearance, personality, and habits).
* **Shopping & Food:** What is your go-to comfort food? How often do you buy groceries or eat out?
* **Weather & Seasons:** Which season do you prefer and why? Describe a typical weather day in your city.

### 🟦 Intermediate (B1–B2)
* **Technology in Daily Life:** How has your smartphone changed your habits over the last few years?
* **Career & Ambitions:** What does an ideal work environment look like to you?
* **Problem Solving:** Think of a minor problem you had this week (e.g., bad traffic, tech issue) and explain how you handled it.
* **Culture & Entertainment:** Compare two movies or music genres. Which one do you prefer and why?
* **Personal Habits:** What is one good habit you want to build this year, and what is one bad habit you want to break?
* **Hypothetical Scenarios:** If you won $10,000 today, how would you spend or invest it?

### 🟪 Advanced (C1–C2)
* **Ethics & Technology:** Should AI be regulated in creative industries like art and writing?
* **Globalization:** How does learning a second language alter your perspective or cultural worldview?
* **Economics & Lifestyle:** Is minimal living superior to consumerism, or does it depend on personal values?
* **Education Reform:** If you could redesign the school curriculum for teenagers, what three subjects would you make mandatory?
* **Abstract Concepts:** Define what "success" means to you today versus what it meant to you five years ago.

---
### Quick 2-Minute Warm-Up Prompts (Any Level)
* **The "Object" Game:** Pick one random item on your desk and explain its history/use for 60 seconds without stopping.
* **Storytelling:** Tell a story about a funny, strange, or awkward moment that happened to you in the past.
* **Pros & Cons:** Pick a random topic (e.g., living in a big city) and list two pros and two cons out loud.
